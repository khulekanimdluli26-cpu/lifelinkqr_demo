LifeLinkQR Access Audit Log

WHAT IT DOES
Every successful professional scan creates:
profiles/{profileId}/accessLogs/{autoId}

Stored fields:
- scannerUid
- scannerName
- scannerRole
- accessedCategory
- categoryLabel
- result
- reason
- scannedAt

PATIENT VIEW
The patient dashboard shows the latest 20 authorised access events.

SECURITY
- Patient owner: can read their own access history.
- Approved professional: can create a scan log, but cannot read, edit, or delete audit logs.
- Administrator: can read and manage audit logs.
- The audit entry contains access metadata, not the protected medical/academic/criminal record itself.

FIRESTORE
Deploy the included firestore.rules only after testing in Firebase Rules Playground.

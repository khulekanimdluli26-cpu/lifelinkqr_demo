LIFELINKQR DEMO42 — STAGE 11: SMALL BUSINESS OPERATING SYSTEM

Built on V10.

New business tools:
- business-dashboard.html: business overview and job counters
- business-profile.html: business identity, contact details and service area
- business-services.html: create/delete services and starting prices
- business-customers.html: create/delete customer records
- professional dashboard now links to Business OS

Firestore collections:
- businesses/{uid}
- businessServices/{serviceId}
- businessCustomers/{customerId}

Security:
- Business profile is owner/admin controlled.
- Business service records are readable by signed-in users; only the owning business can create/update/delete them.
- Business customer records are private to the owning business.
- No Firebase Storage is used.
- Existing Stage 10 duplicate serviceRequests rules were merged into one rule.

Important:
Deploy Firestore rules only:
"%APPDATA%\\npm\\firebase.cmd" deploy --only firestore:rules
Do NOT run full Firebase deploy while Storage billing/setup is unavailable.

Stage 11 is the foundation for:
employees/team management, invoices, expenses, calendar, analytics, AI assistant and payments in Stage 12/production.

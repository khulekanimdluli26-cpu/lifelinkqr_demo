PATIENT OWNER PORTAL

1. Open dashboard.html after logging in.
2. Click “Manage My Information”.
3. The portal loads the logged-in user's profileId from users/{uid}.
4. The patient can manage civilian/emergency, medical and academic records.
5. Criminal history is intentionally not editable by the patient.
6. The scanner still uses role-specific collections only.

IMPORTANT FIRESTORE MODEL
- Patient owner: read/write own civilian, medical and academic records.
- Patient owner: cannot edit criminal history.
- Approved doctor: read medical only.
- Approved educator: read academic only.
- Approved police: read criminal only.
- Approved civilian: read civilian/emergency only.
- Admin: manage protected records.

Deploy firestore.rules only after reviewing/testing it in the Firebase Rules simulator.

LifeLinkQR Access History finalisation

- Patient dashboard displays audit timestamps as readable South African dates/times.
- Administrator activity uses the same readable timestamp format.
- Raw Firestore timestamp objects are not intentionally rendered by the dashboard/admin views.
- Audit records remain under profiles/{profileId}/accessLogs.
- Patient owners can read their own access history; approved scanners can create authorised logs; admins can manage logs according to Firestore rules.

Test:
1. Scan the same QR with Doctor, Educator, Police and Emergency roles.
2. Return to the patient dashboard.
3. Confirm four access-history entries appear with professional, category, result and readable date/time.
4. Open Admin and confirm the same activity appears there.
5. If a raw timestamp object appears anywhere, identify the exact screen/field so its renderer can be patched.

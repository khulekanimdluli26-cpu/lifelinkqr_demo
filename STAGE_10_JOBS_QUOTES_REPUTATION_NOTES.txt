LifeLinkQR Stage 10 — Jobs, Quotes, Bookings & Reputation

Implemented:
- Professional jobs dashboard for incoming customer requests
- Professional can submit quote amount and quote note
- Professional can update request status: requested, quoted, accepted, in_progress, completed, cancelled
- Customer can see updated status and review a completed job
- Customer review page with 1–5 star rating and written review
- Review security requires the reviewer to own the completed service request
- Duplicate review prevention in the UI
- professionalName captured when a customer creates a request
- No Firebase Storage required
- Firestore rules expanded for Stage 10

Test:
1. Customer submits a service request.
2. Professional opens professional-jobs.html.
3. Professional enters quote and selects quoted.
4. Customer refreshes My Service Requests and sees quoted.
5. Professional can move job through accepted -> in_progress -> completed.
6. Customer opens Review Professional and submits rating/review.

Deploy only Firestore rules:
"%APPDATA%\\npm\\firebase.cmd" deploy --only firestore:rules

LifeLinkQR Verification Management V5

Added:
- Admin Verification Management page.
- Search by name/email/role/profile ID.
- Filter by verification status and role.
- View professional details in a popup.
- Approve/reject controls.
- Approval sets verificationStatus=approved and accountStatus=active.
- Rejection sets verificationStatus=rejected and accountStatus=pending.

Security note:
The page checks admins/{uid} before loading users. Firestore rules must continue to restrict writes to administrators.
